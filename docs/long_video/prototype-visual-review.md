# MEMORY_LAB_EDITORIAL Prototype Visual Review

## Review evidence

- Reviewed outputs: the ten current MP4 files in `outputs/extreme-long-video/prototypes/`.
- Review 2 evidence: `outputs/extreme-long-video/prototypes/review2-mobile/` contains 120 retained PNGs, sampled at 2 fps and scaled to 480×270 (12 frames for each approximately six-second prototype).
- The contact sheet was checked as an index only. Motion semantics and persistence were judged from the videos and sequential frame sets, not from a single still.
- Audio is silent at approximately -91 dB by design. These files are design previews, not narration acceptance evidence.

## Independent review 1

The first review found P1 semantic or evidence problems in 02, 06, 07, 08, 09, and 10:

- 02 did not show the physical source behind the Bartlett story chain.
- 06 did not preserve the immediate-confidence T0 baseline before feedback.
- 07 did not keep T0 and confidence/consistency distinctions explicit.
- 08 reused a confidence-feedback grammar instead of showing eyewitness contamination.
- 09 read as an automatic upgrade funnel.
- 10 did not complete an external-evidence verification path.

All six were revised before review 2.

## Full-render gate

The prototypes do not authorize a production render by themselves. The current preview asset map contains six semantically exact bindings (`S015`, `S022`, `S053`, `S078`, `S079`, and `S098`) for 19 archive requirements. `S090` has no binding; a previously generated mapping to the Doty family portrait was removed as semantically wrong. Thirteen requirements remain unbound until their exact source, physical file, rights, and narrative role are all closed.

A first full render is allowed only after exact narration/audio manifests, all required asset bindings, the deployed `memory-lab-editorial@1` renderer, and reconciliation gates are present.
