# MEMORY_LAB_EDITORIAL

## Concept

Memory is not a video file. It is a working archive whose current version is assembled from traces, meanings, source judgments, later information, and repeated telling.

The film should feel like a quiet editorial investigation conducted in a dark evidence room, with paper archive and contemporary cognitive laboratory materials brought forward only when the narration needs them. It must not resemble a file manager, AI dashboard, cyber-brain HUD, a recolored R3-D Freud composition, or a light template simply inverted to black.

## Visual language

- Canvas: deep charcoal / blue-grey, warm white, low-saturation cyan, subdued coral red. Light paper exists only as an archive, experimental material, quotation, or document layer.
- Typography: Chinese-first; `visualThesis` is a 6–18-character visual proposition (two lines maximum), and `visualLabels` are compact terms. Narration text remains reserved for TTS/subtitles and cannot become the large title.
- Subtitle safety: the main thesis, labels, and source markers remain above the lower 224 px; subtitles own that lower region.
- Evidence labels: `原始研究`, `后续证据`, `解释`, `边界`. They are claims of role, not confidence scores.
- Motion: rapid state change followed by a readable hold. Static frames are allowed.
- Camera: no ambient drift. Pan or crop only to move from a source to the exact detail being discussed.

## Five chapter worlds

| World | Chapters | Purpose | Dominant material |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OPEN FILE | 1 and 10 | Establish and resolve the “versioned archive” metaphor | photograph fragments, source tabs, external verification |
| HISTORY DESK | 2 | Trace Bartlett, the story source chain, and replication limits | public-domain book pages, annotated provenance, two-method timeline |
| EXPERIMENT ROOM | 3, 4 and 6 | Make controlled manipulations and measurements visible | stimulus/result staging, word-list demo, confidence timing |
| RECONSTRUCTION TABLE | 5 and 7 | Compare mechanisms and longitudinal records | gist/detail layers, source attribution, candidate mechanisms, T0 retests |
| REAL-WORLD RECORD | 8 and 9 | Show legal procedure and autobiographical-memory classification | official process, preserved first statement, belief-to-memory funnel |

## Nine visual families

1. **KINETIC_CLAIM** — a short claim or question with a local word-level change.
2. **VERSION_DIFF** — two versions overlap, are covered, or visibly diverge.
3. **PROCESS_MAP** — only for a real causal or staged relation.
4. **TIMELINE** — only for real time points; advance local view or camera state within a sequence.
5. **EVIDENCE_ARCHIVE** — authentic material fills a useful area; crop, push, mask, or annotate the existing source rather than floating a small card.
6. **CONCEPT_SPACE** — distance, overlap, hierarchy, and aggregation express a relation without inventing a process.
7. **COMPARISON** — a genuine two-variable or two-explanation contrast, not two default bars.
8. **CHAPTER_INTERSTITIAL** — chapter number, one short question, and one visual symbol only.
9. **FINAL_SYNTHESIS** — end-only collection of material types and final proposition; never the generic five-block layout.

## Scene-state contract

Each production scene corresponds to one deterministic speech unit because the current V2 visual compiler requires equal counts. A scene may contain two or more visual beats, but later beats must preserve the completed state of earlier beats.

For every visible motion:

```text
actor
source
target
reason
visible result
```

If any field is missing, remove the motion.

### Allowed persistence patterns

- A new source layer is added beside the original; the original does not disappear.
- A confidence bar changes only after feedback appears; the first recorded statement stays pinned.
- A later flashbulb-memory report appears beside T0; T0 is never rewritten.
- In the DRM demonstration, the missing lure may illuminate at test, but it must never be inserted into the earlier presented list.
- In the autobiographical-memory funnel, a report can move only to the category supported by the narration; no automatic climb to “full memory.”

### Forbidden resets

- cards re-entering on the second sentence;
- paths retracting after completion;
- the camera snapping back to a default wide shot at a subtitle boundary;
- evidence labels changing role without a narrated reason;
- archival photographs disappearing merely to make room for a new caption;
- a scene beginning with a completed state that contradicts the previous scene’s handoff.

## Repetition budget

- No family may occupy more than two consecutive speech scenes or hold the same composition for more than about 18 seconds.
- No world may run longer than about 150 seconds without a material or explanatory change.
- The same transition type may repeat at most twice consecutively.
- Archive assets may reappear only when the narration refers back to that exact source.
- A layout variation counts only when hierarchy or explanatory action changes; moving the same cards to new coordinates does not count.
- Chapter boundaries may use a stable title convention, but chapter title cards must be brief and must not reset a sentence already in progress.

## Prototype set

Before a full render, review representative intervals for:

1. opening fragment assembly;
2. Bartlett provenance chain and replication limitation;
3. Loftus wording manipulation;
4. DRM missing-word demonstration;
5. reconsolidation as one candidate among several;
6. initial-confidence versus feedback timeline;
7. flashbulb T0 comparison;
8. eyewitness safeguards;
9. belief/memory classification funnel;
10. final external-verification synthesis.

Each prototype must be watched at normal speed and checked at mobile scale. Contact sheets alone cannot pass semantic motion or pacing.
